Welcome to De Wisdom Comprehensive Academy

De Wisdom Comprehensive Academy was established with a singular vision: of making quality and effective Western Education accessible, affordable, enticing and learner's friendly. De Wisdom Comprehensive Academy is privileged to be the first private school in Ageva (1998), and the first educational institution in Zariagi (1999). Approved for teaching and learning in 2003 by the Kogi State Ministry of Education. Approved for WAEC and NECO Exams in 2006.

Over two decades De Wisdom Comprehensive Academy has progressively metamorphosed:

-Setting the pace

-Mentoring

-Fostering Sports and Culture

-Embracing Science, technology and Inovation.

Where we are now is worth reckoning. But where we are going it the most targeted.

Our Institutional History

De Wisdom Comprehensive Academy was established 26th October, 1998 at Ageva Community, in Ageva Local Development Area by Salawu Ajayi David. Zariagi became a target, subsequent upon the original intention, ideals, aims and objectives of making quality and effective Western Education accessible, affordable, enticing and learners' friendly, in educationally less developed communities. Hence the installation of a study centre in 1999, now De Wisdom Comprehensive Academy, Zariagi. De Wisdom is previleged to be the first private school in Ageva (1998), and the first educational institution in Zariagi (1999). Approved for teaching and learning in 2003 by the Kogi State Ministry of education. Approved for WAEC & NECO Exams in 2006.

De Wisdom has progressively metamorphosed:

- setting the pace

- mentoring

- fostering sports & culture

- embracing science, technology and innovation.

Where we are now is worthy of reckoning. But where we are going is the most targeted.
